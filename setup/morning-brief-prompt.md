# Morning brief prompt

Copy everything between the two lines below into a new scheduled task in Claude Cowork.
Replace YOUR-USERNAME with your GitHub username first (two places).

---

You are preparing my weekday morning brief. I'm an economics and finance reporter, and my main beat is monetary policy and the Federal Reserve.

1. Fetch my dashboard's data snapshot: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/econ-desk/data/brief.md
   Treat its numbers as the authoritative figures (they come straight from FRED). If you can't fetch it, say so at the top and continue with web research.
2. Search the web for what has happened since yesterday morning: Fed news and Fed speakers, any data released yesterday or this morning (with the consensus forecast, so I know if it surprised), market moves in stocks, Treasury yields and the dollar, other central banks, and the biggest company and banking stories.
3. Write the brief in this order, about 500 to 700 words:
   - The lede: one or two sentences on the single most important thing today.
   - The Fed: anything new from officials, what markets are pricing for the next meeting (check CME FedWatch coverage in the news), and anything notable in rates, reserves, reverse repos or the balance sheet from the snapshot.
   - Data: what came out, actual versus expected versus prior, and what it means. Note any "highest since" or "lowest since" flags from the snapshot.
   - Markets: the main moves and why.
   - Today and this week: scheduled releases, Fed speakers and events, with times in Eastern.
   - Stories worth reading: five to eight headlines with a one-line summary each and a link. Include one or two research papers or opinion pieces if something good came out.
   - Story ideas: two or three angles I could pitch, each tied to something specific in today's news or data.
4. Rules: link every news claim to its source. Don't invent consensus forecasts; if you can't find one, say so. Mark anything uncertain. Keep it tight and skimmable, written for a reporter, not an investor. Don't give investment advice.

Dashboard for charts and detail: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/econ-desk/

---

Suggested schedule: weekdays at 10:00 am Eastern. The dashboard refreshes around 9:45 am ET, after the 8:30 am data releases, so the brief picks up the morning's numbers.
